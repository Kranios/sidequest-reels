# SideQuest — 10 Reel Themes (test → double down)

Goal: app-store downloads (iOS launch 2026). Ratio target ≈ 70% emotion/story,
30% app-demo. App is the solution that appears ~3s in, never the opening.

Themes grouped into 3 categories so double-down is measurable — each category
tests a different psychological driver.

## A. Surprise-reveal (unique feature — highest virality potential)
1. **POV: your friend planned a secret sidequest** — blur → countdown → reveal.
2. **I hid a surprise for my partner the whole trip** — story, relationship angle.
3. **The reveal hit at the exact right moment** — timing mechanic focus.

## B. Relatable group problem (broad reach)
4. **Who owes who after the trip?** — cost split, real € data.
5. **Every group has THAT person who plans nothing** — humor, roles.
6. **5 apps → 1** — reworked, snappier, with motion.

## C. Aspirational / tracker (save + share friendly)
7. **Countries visited vs planned** — 3D globe filling in.
8. **Plan a 5-day trip in 30 seconds** — speedrun through the flow.
9. **Trips I'm manifesting for 2026** — globe + empty trips, dream angle.
10. **What a perfectly planned trip looks like** — aspirational aesthetic.

## Batch 1 (build first)
- Theme 1 (reveal) — priority, only feature no competitor has, algo-favored format
- Theme 7 (globe) — save-friendly, low production cost
- Theme 4 (cost split) — needs split route capture working

## Measurement (don't double-down on views alone)
| Signal | Measures | Winning category tends to be |
|---|---|---|
| Reach | hook works | any (tells you which opening lands) |
| Saves + shares | value / aspiration | tracker (C) |
| Profile visits → app clicks | actual conversion | the only one that drives downloads |

Process: 2 reels/theme for ~3 weeks → compare per category first, then per reel
within the winning category → build 4-6 more on the winner.

## Per-reel tracking columns (see analytics_template.csv)
reel_id, theme, category, posted_date, views, reach, saves, shares,
profile_visits, link_clicks, notes
