# Higgsfield — Credit Budget & Model Guide

Higgsfield's ONLY job in this pipeline is b-roll + hook footage. Not app UI
(that's Playwright capture), not the phone (that's Blender). So credit burn is
low if you stay disciplined. This file is the plan for spending as little as
possible while testing batch 1.

## The rules that save credits
1. **Draft at 720p, finish at 1080p.** Resolution roughly doubles the burn
   (~14cr for a 5s clip at 720p vs ~45cr at 1080p on the pricier models).
   Only re-render the winning clip at 1080p.
2. **Monthly credits do NOT roll over** — unused credits reset at month end.
   Don't subscribe until you're ready to actually spend the month's pool.
3. **Top-up packs expire ~90 days** — buy them for a known overage, not "just in case."
4. **Not every reel needs b-roll.** Tracker (C) and split (B) themes work with
   zero Higgsfield. Spend credits where footage actually lifts the reel.
5. **Iteration is the hidden cost.** Assume ~2 attempts per usable clip. Budget
   for it, don't be surprised by it.

## Model choice
- **Draft / most b-roll:** a low-cost model (e.g. Kling-class, ~14cr per 5s @720p).
- **Finish (winning clip only):** step up resolution to 1080p, accept the ~2x.
- Avoid the premium flagship models (Sora/Veo-class) for this use — they burn
  3-5x more and the footage is background b-roll, not the hero.
- Nano-Banana-class image gen is ~pocket change (~2cr) if you ever need a still.

## Batch 1 estimate (themes 1, 7, 4)
- Theme 1 hook: 1 usable clip × ~2 attempts × 14cr (720p draft) = ~28cr
- Finish that clip once at 1080p = ~45cr
- Buffer for a second angle = ~30cr
- **Batch 1 total ≈ 100-120 credits.** Your trial pool covers a test clip;
  the full batch likely needs a starter month.

## Full 10-theme rough estimate
~15 clips × 2 attempts × 14cr (720p) ≈ 420cr, + 1080p finals on ~6 winners
(~270cr) ≈ **~700 credits** across the whole test phase. Spread over 1-2 months.

## Plan decision (don't over-commit)
1. **Now:** spend remaining trial credits on ONE theme-1 hook clip. Run it
   through compose. Decide if b-roll actually beats plain capture.
2. **If yes:** cheapest annual plan (~$15-19/mo, ~200-270cr) covers the test
   phase (~10-15 clips/mo). Track cost-per-usable-clip in week 1.
3. **Scale only if** a theme takes off and you want b-roll-heavy reels. Prefer
   a top-up pack over jumping a tier for one busy month.
4. If you ever need plain text-to-video only, price Runway/Kling direct first —
   may be cheaper per clip. Higgsfield's value here is breadth + the MCP bridge.
